Field notes for the CEO

The Smith and the Tailor

By Marcos Stipicich · 9 November 2026

Many deployments are cut before anyone measures the floor, by someone who will not be there when the seams give.

Call that person the smith. The smith arrives with a tool, frames every problem in its terms and leaves with a sign-off. The tailor does the opposite: measures before cutting, lets the machine sew the long seams, fits by hand and comes back for the second fitting. They are not two kinds of people. The same strategist can be a smith on Monday and a tailor on Thursday.

The field notes end with two rules a CEO can set before kickoff: name the owner of the change in writing, on your side and not the deployer, and run the 90-day check.
