**Three things I ask for.** The floor, including the off shifts, under your site rules. The raw records, in five columns: case, activity, timestamp, who, where. One executive who can unblock.

**Contracting.** I work as an independent contractor from Chile. Your contractor agreement or mine, invoices in USD, payment by wire or Wise, or through Deel or Remote if you prefer. W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E on request, depending on how the contract is set up. No payroll, benefits or employer contributions on your side.

**On site.** No work-visa sponsorship is needed to bring me to your site. I travel on business-visitor entry, with fees invoiced from Chile.

- United States: ESTA under the Visa Waiver Program, or a B-1 visa, up to 90 days per trip.
- Australia: Visitor visa (subclass 600), Business Visitor stream, up to three months.
- Canada: eTA.
- United Kingdom: ETA.
- Schengen area: visa-free short stays.

I come for meetings, floor audits, workshops and cutover training. I lived and worked in Melbourne and hold a current Victorian forklift licence (class LF).
