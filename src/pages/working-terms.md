**Three things I ask for.** The floor, including the off shifts, under your site rules. The raw records, in five columns: case, activity, timestamp, who, where. One executive who can unblock.

**On the floor.** Visits are floor-first, not conference-room interviews. I wear your required PPE, walk every shift, and speak directly with operators, shift leads, planners, and managers so nothing is filtered before it reaches the baseline. I work in plain sight under your site rules and safety induction; I do not run your equipment, fill an operational post, or bypass your management chain.

**Field notes and capture.** To keep my eyes on the operation rather than a notebook, I dictate observations into a pocket recorder and take short clips of screens, labels, and racks, never faces, and an operator is recorded only if they agree. Captures are working notes to build the timeline and check counts, not a video deliverable. They stay under your NDA, never leave the project, and are deleted on closeout unless your contract requires handover. Where site rules prohibit recording, I work strictly from paper notes and system exports.

**After the work.** Every audit and every go-live includes a check by video 90 days later. If the measures we agreed in writing before kickoff have not held, I work with your team to bring them back, with no fee for that work; travel, if needed, is at cost. This assumes the three things I ask for above were in place. Returning clients pay 20% less for advisory hours and on-site days for 12 months after an engagement ends.

**Contracting.** I work as an independent contractor from Chile. All technical execution, data audits, deterministic guardrails and remote deployment support are performed from Chile and invoiced in USD (payment by wire, Wise, Deel or Remote; W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E on request). No payroll, benefits or employer contributions on your side.

**On-site alignment.** When an engagement includes short visits for executive alignment, commercial scoping or floor observation within business-visitor rules, I travel on business-visitor entry:

- United States: ESTA under the Visa Waiver Program, or a B-1 visa, up to 90 days per trip.
- Australia: Visitor visa (subclass 600), Business Visitor stream, up to three months.
- Canada: eTA.
- United Kingdom: ETA.
- Schengen area: visa-free short stays.

Hands-on execution is delivered remotely from Chile or under the appropriate work authorisation when a longer on-site deployment requires it. I lived and worked in Melbourne and hold a current Victorian forklift licence (class LF).
