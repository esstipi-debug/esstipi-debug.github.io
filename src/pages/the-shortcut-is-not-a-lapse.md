On a picking line, the dented unit ships because reporting it costs the picker their own number, and shipping it costs someone else, later.

It is often called a lapse and answered with a campaign. The arithmetic suggests otherwise. When doing it right costs the person who writes the record minutes against the metric they are judged on, and doing it wrong costs them nothing, the shortcut is not a failure of character. It is the predictable choice, and in my experience it outlives awareness training.

The field notes end with three questions to ask where the record is written, before blaming the people who write it.
