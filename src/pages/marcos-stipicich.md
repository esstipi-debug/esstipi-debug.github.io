I grew up on a sheep ranch in Patagonia, where my father's rule was simple: reading is worth nothing if you can't see it on a working day. I still work by it: I measure before I cut, and no tool or method beats what works on the floor.

Thirteen years in Chile, Argentina and Australia taught me that supply chain is not just logistics: it is the whole pipeline, which is why it ends up putting out fires that start elsewhere. A promotion planned in marketing becomes expedited orders in purchasing and a blocked aisle in the warehouse. I would rather find where the leak starts. On one gemba walk, mixed, short and damaged orders led back to a per-operator productivity KPI tied to a bonus; I retired the metric and measured customer satisfaction instead.

I have worked most of that pipeline: planning for more than 200 stores, a three-site logistics operation, a USD 30M cross-border e-commerce P&L and a 50,000-SKU warehouse-system cutover, where I stayed on the floor until the shortcuts died and order processing time fell 20%. Deployments live or die inside that pipeline, and that is where I work. I learned to code in C; today I build with AI coding agents, keeping the math and the rollback in my hands.

Available for pre-cutover forensic audits, deployment strategy, and Forward Deployed roles.
