An approval only controls what the approver would catch. When errors are rare, people can miss more of them, and the log looks the same.

A person reviews every purchase order and payment the system proposes. The log proves the review happened, not that a wrong one would have been caught. A wrong payment or PO that clears this queue is a loss the signature will not explain to your auditors. If that approval is why the system went live, the deployment rests on a control with an unknown catch rate.

The field notes end with what airports do instead: plant fake threats and count who finds them. Test the control, not the signature.
