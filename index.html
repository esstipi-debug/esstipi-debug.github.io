Short answers on what happens to systems and AI after go-live, each with one mechanism and one source, from thirteen years on warehouse floors and in planning.

Questions

  • Who should own adoption after an ERP or WMS go-live?An executive on your side, named in writing before kickoff, who is not the deployer. Without one, the change after go-live belongs to nobody.
  • Why do deployers apply the same tool to every problem?Because knowing a tool shapes what you see, even for experts. The safeguard is a rule set before kickoff, not the deployer's open mind.
  • Should you customize the ERP or change the process to fit it?Neither by default. Measure the misfit first, sort what the system lacks from what it imposes, and weigh each customization against its upkeep.
  • How do you check a deployment 90 days after go-live?Log twenty system recommendations a month against what the operator would do and what doing nothing costs. If neither ever wins, audit the deployer.
  • Why do people follow the system even when it is wrong?Because obeying costs them nothing today and questioning has a price. What reduces the error is making people answer for the decision.
  • Do manual overrides of a statistical forecast improve accuracy?Sometimes: large adjustments tend to help and small ones often hurt. Without a name and a reason, a company cannot learn which overrides paid off.
  • Should the reason for an override be recorded before or after the change?Before, and for a reviewer whose view the operator does not know. A reason written after the fact is a defence; one written to please is an echo.
  • Why don’t inventory records match the shelf, and what does that do to a model?In one retailer's audit, 65% of nearly 370,000 records did not match the shelf. A model trained on them is wrong exactly where the record is.
  • A customer says “not received” and the system says “delivered”: which record is wrong?Often neither. In a public marketplace dataset, 57% of "not received" reviews on delivered orders were written on or before the delivery day.
