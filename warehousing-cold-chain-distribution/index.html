Industry

Warehousing, cold chain and distribution

The system says the pallet is in the location. The picker says it isn’t. Both are doing their job.

What I check first

A blind count of a sample of locations, without showing the expected balance. Whether logins are personal or shared: if they are shared, the system cannot say who moved what. What the bonus pays for. Where the money sits: stock that has not moved, and what it costs to keep it cold.

Where I have stood

Planning for more than 200 stores, with a team of 15 planners. A three-site logistics operation with about 100 people and a USD 15M budget. A USD 30M cross-border e-commerce P&L, where about USD 5M of inventory was freed. A USD 100M inventory position, as its analyst. A 50,000-SKU warehouse-system changeover (the record). Employers before 2022 are described, not named, under confidentiality.

What I don’t claim

I don’t promise a percentage before I have counted. The first number you get from me is how much of your record is true.

How we would start

From your own exports first: movements, counts and adjustments for the last few months. Then a blind count of one zone, on site.
