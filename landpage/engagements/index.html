After the work

The 90-day check is included. Ninety days after an audit is delivered or a system goes live, we check by video whether it held. After an audit, your team re-counts the same sample and we see whether the losses on the list came down. After a go-live, the 90-day check: who still uses the system, who went around it, and why.

If it did not hold, putting it back is on me. The work to bring the agreed measures back carries no fee; travel, if it is needed, is at cost. The measures are the ones we agree in writing before we start.

Coming back costs less. For 12 months after an engagement ends, advisory hours and on-site days cost 20% less.
