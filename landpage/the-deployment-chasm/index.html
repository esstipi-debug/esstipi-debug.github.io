The Deployment Chasm

Manuscript

By Marcos Stipicich

The Deployment Chasm is a manuscript by Marcos Stipicich on why enterprise technology that works in the plan fails on the operational floor, from ISO 9000 and ERP to generative AI. It is written from thirteen years in supply chain and operations.

It runs to 551 pages: fourteen chapters, five appendices and a bibliography of 130 sources.

The sample (PDF, 7 pages) has the cover, the contents and an excerpt from chapter 1: the three seams between the system and the floor, and how to measure the pressure to work around the system in one week.

The full text is not public. Some of its pieces appear, one mechanism at a time, in the field notes on this site.
