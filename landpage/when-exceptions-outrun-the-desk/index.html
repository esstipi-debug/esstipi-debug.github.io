When Exceptions Outrun the Desk

Field notes for the COO

By Marcos Stipicich · 16 November 2026

When exceptions reach the desk faster than its people can resolve them, the overflow does not stop the dock. It leaves the procedure.

It surfaces weeks later as dirty data, and it is often answered with a reminder about discipline. Exceptions arrive at the rate the trucks bring them, and the desk clears them at the rate its people can. When the first rate is higher, the queue grows every shift and something has to give. The dock has a truck waiting; the inventory record has until month-end. A business case that takes a person off the desk without taking exceptions off the dock has already chosen which one gives.

The field notes end with four numbers to keep each week, to read before the next headcount decision and before the new system writes more to the record on its own.
