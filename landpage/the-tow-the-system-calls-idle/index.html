The Tow the System Calls Idle

Field notes for the COO

By Marcos Stipicich · 16 November 2026

A forklift stuck on frost in a cold store took two drivers and a tow strap to free. The record called it idle, or a slow driver.

It is easy to file that under bad luck, or under a standard that needs tuning. My reading is that the standard was missing a category. A system can only book a minute under a name it has. Standards carry delay codes and allowances for what someone put on the list; the trouble is the fact that is not on it. Its minutes do not disappear. They go to whatever category is left, and at that site that was idle time or below standard, charged to the person who was driving.

The field notes end with five steps to let the floor contest the record. Count the reasons that come back: each one is a name the standard is missing.
