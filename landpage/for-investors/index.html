Investors

For investors buying or backing a physical operation

The data room says the inventory is worth what the balance sheet says. Somebody should count a sample before the price is set.

What I check first

Record accuracy, by blind sample. Whether shrink and spoilage are recorded when they happen or at period end. What runs on the system and what runs on a spreadsheet beside it. What the floor is paid for.

Where I have stood

Thirteen years on the operating side: planning for more than 200 stores, a three-site logistics operation, a USD 30M cross-border e-commerce P&L, and a USD 100M inventory position as its analyst. I grew up on sheep stations and have worked in professional kitchens, so farms and kitchens are floors I have stood on too.

What I don’t claim

I have not worked in a fund. I am the operator you send to look.

How we would start

A records audit from the data-room exports, then a site visit to count a sample before the price is set. Or an hour on a call before you decide.
